Compacts your own conversation history before your next model call. Your older messages are replaced by a summary that keeps your name, the other agents, the goal, the messages you received and sent, your promises to other agents, the files changed, your work in progress, your decisions, and your next steps. Your newest work stays in your history word for word, and your system instructions and the goal are never removed. Call it when you finish a phase of work or when your history grows long, so that you keep room for the work ahead. Compaction also happens automatically when your history grows long, so you never need to call it twice in a row. It applies only when there is older history to compact, and the call itself returns at once. The files in the workplace are not changed by compaction, so anything you wrote there stays available.
