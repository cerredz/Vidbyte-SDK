Updates your goal card, which sits at the top of your context on every call and survives when older parts of this conversation are removed. Only three sections can change: the plan and the blockers are replaced by the text you send, and progress gains one fact for each non-blank line of the text. The goal and the done criteria are fixed for the run and cannot be patched. A successful call returns the name of the section it changed, and the card shows the change from your next call on. A text over a section's size cap is refused with the reason, and a full progress section is refused with a note that says so.
