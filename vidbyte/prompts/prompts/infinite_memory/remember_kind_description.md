## Purpose

What kind of knowledge the entry records, which tells a later agent how to use it.

## How to choose

Choose decision for a choice that was made, with its reason when it fits. Choose fact for something established, ideally with its source in refs. Choose lesson for what to do or avoid next time, open_question for something still unknown that a later agent should resolve, and pointer for an entry whose job is to name a workspace file that holds longer material.

## Rules

The kind must be one of the values named here. When an entry could fit two kinds, pick the one that best tells a reader what to do with it.
