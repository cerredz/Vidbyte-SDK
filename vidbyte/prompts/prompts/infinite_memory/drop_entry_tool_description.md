## What it does

Drops one memory entry that this pair staged, whether the generator or you staged it, so that it is never saved.

## When to use it

Review every staged entry against the evidence before you give your verdict. Drop an entry that is wrong, unsupported, too vague to stand alone, not worth keeping, or a duplicate of a live or staged entry. When its knowledge still matters, stage a corrected entry with remember after dropping the flawed one.

## How it behaves

Name the entry by its staged key, as listed in your first message or returned by remember. The reply lists the keys still staged, followed by your quota line. Every entry you leave staged is saved once your final reply carries a verdict, whatever the verdict, while a reply without a verdict saves none of them.

## Rules and refusals

Only entries staged in this pair can be dropped; an entry already saved is corrected through remember with supersedes. A key that is not currently staged is refused with the list of keys that are. A drop cannot be undone.
