## Purpose

The entry itself: one short line that makes sense on its own to an agent that has never seen this conversation.

## How to write it

Record one idea per entry. Name the subject plainly instead of pointing back at earlier messages, and include the specific value, name, or condition that makes the entry useful. Write it as a statement a later reader can act on, and keep supporting sources in refs rather than in the text.

## Rules

The text must fit on one line and stay under the entry size limit. Text already saved or staged, ignoring case and spacing, is refused. Keep longer material in a note written with write_note, and remember a pointer entry that names the note.
