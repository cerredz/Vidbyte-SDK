## What the implementation gets right (so nobody "fixes" it)
- **The §4 snippet works as written.** With only `VIDBYTE_API_KEY` set, it prints `openai gpt-5.5` / `Hi` / `2` and sends exactly one POST to `…/api/v1/models/generate`. That POST carries the three INV-3 headers, a 32-hex `Idempotency-Key` equal to the `idempotency_key` argument, timeout 630.0, and the exact §4 envelope with `max_output_tokens: 8192`. Building the explicit-managed `claude` runner sends nothing and pins `VIDBYTE_API_KEY` even when `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` is set. Keep the pinning in `TextModelRunner.__init__` (`text.py:46-54`) and the per-call wrap inside async `arun` (`text.py:77-84`).
- **Key selection fails closed and follows INV-4:**
  - kimi reads `MOONSHOT_API_KEY`; a blank explicit key falls through; `" sk-x "` stays BYOK;
  - openrouter ignores `VIDBYTE_API_KEY`; a `vb_live_` key in `OPENAI_API_KEY` goes managed;
  - explicit managed mode never reads the provider env var;
  - none of these sends a request: a malformed `VIDBYTE_API_KEY`, an `sk-` key in `VIDBYTE_API_KEY`, a `vb_live_` key with an endpoint, or with an unmanaged provider;
  - adapters only ever see `VIDBYTE_MANAGED_KEY_PLACEHOLDER`.
- **Decision delegation is exact.** `resolve_vidbyte_api_key` is shared with `DecisionModelConfig`, and its outcomes and messages for blank, padded, 31-character and `vb_test_` keys equal base. C020 drops from 4 to 2 honestly.
- **The raise shape is safe:**
  - `_send` keeps only the exception class, never the instance (`vidbyte_gateway.py:198-206`);
  - `_json_object` decodes in its own narrow `try`;
  - every error is raised after all `except` blocks, so `__cause__` and `__context__` are `None` and `response_excerpt` is `None`;
  - messages come from status and shape only;
  - a relayed `{"error": {...}}` and `"error": "true"` are not read as the gateway's `"error": true`.
  Mutations of the row-1/row-2 split, the truthiness check, an inner retry and a fresh key per attempt are each caught by the pack.
- **The retry loop matches r5:**
  - one uuid4 key per call;
  - headers and envelope built once and reused;
  - the deadline taken once from `loop.time()` before the first POST;
  - every attempt gets the full timeout, with no `retry_count`;
  - only 409, 502 or a non-JSON-object answer is re-sent, after waits of 0.5 s and 1.0 s.
  The `for … else` re-send counting is correct; do not "simplify" it. Under a fake clock, steps of 0, 600 and 629.5 s give 3, 2 and 1 POSTs with the right messages.
- **BYOK stays isolated.** Only managed configs are pinned. Each call builds its own decorator, so concurrent calls share no state. `resolve_api_key` is untouched, and the AC-3 goldens pass.
- **Boundaries and budget hold:**
  - the 9 production `.py` files changed are all under `vidbyte/lib/`;
  - nothing changed under `vidbyte/providers/` or `vidbyte/agents/`, and `pyproject.toml`, `contracts/` and `vidbyte/lib/http/__init__.py` are unchanged;
  - §8.4 is met exactly: 2 files, 3 classes, 1 field + 8 constants;
  - INV-15 holds: there is no markup or fee constant.
- **Placement and exports are right.** `TextModelMode` is in `vidbyte/lib/enums/text_model.py`, exported from `vidbyte.lib.enums` and re-exported from `vidbyte.lib.config`. `VidbyteGatewayTransport` is not re-exported (R-10). The 8 constants are in `vidbyte/lib/constants/jev.py`, and no new dataclass or error class was added.
- **The lint-driven shapes are honest:**
  - S064 compares the real `loop.time() + retry_wait >= deadline`;
  - C017 traces `bearer_headers(...)` plus a subscript `Idempotency-Key`;
  - A007 is met through `HTTPStatus` and the existing `JEV_*` constants;
  - `gateway_reason` uses dict dispatch;
  - there are no suppressions, and no rule count rose against `e91cf822` (A002 658→653, C012 24→23, C020 4→2).
- **Docs and hygiene are in order:**
  - every added `#` comment line is at most 72 columns;
  - both new modules have full A001 headers, and the "Key Functions" inventories were updated;
  - the contract is a byte-exact copy (blob `56d4d0b3`, no BOM) with its Provenance row and Change Log line;
  - commits are split per §12.3 row and cite IDs, the C020 ratchet is its own commit, and no secrets are committed;
  - S2 test files are byte-identical to `2eb7e3e5`.
