## What the implementation gets right (so nobody "fixes" it)
- **TypeSafe regression safety.** The managed branches of `vidbyte/providers/typesafe.py` are byte-identical to main. Direct mode reaches System One only through `SystemOneProvider.HOSTS[ModelProvider.TYPESAFE]`, `SystemOneRequest`, `SystemOneAnswers` and `DecisionFailures`. The 7 regression tests and the 406-test area suite pass. Do not re-split the relocated classes.
- **The config guards are exact.** The unsupported-provider message lists all 16 providers sorted. Managed mode for any non-TypeSafe provider raises "VIDBYTE_MANAGED decision mode is available only for provider typesafe." (`model_configs.py:376`). `validate()` resolves the key, then the endpoint (`:402`). `model` is filled in `__post_init__` and read through `resolved_model()` (`:410`). EC-1, EC-2, EC-4, EC-20, EC-24 and AC-8 print the spec's exact messages.
- **INV-30 ordering is explicit and pinned by index.** `baseten/inception/mercury-decide` comes before `inception/mercury-decide` in `vidbyte/lib/constants/runners.py`. So `provider_for_model("inception/mercury-decide")` returns baseten, `"mercury-decide"` returns inception, and `(BASETEN, "mercury-decide")` raises the ownership message word for word. `kai`, `instinct`, `clef-flash` and `d1` route to their own hosts. Do not reorder the catalog.
- **The pricebook matches §9.1 cell for cell, and its math is right.** There are 13 new decision blocks plus the `gpt-6-luna` tier (threshold 272,000, 0.20 above it, `threshold_inclusive=False`). At 272,000 input tokens the cost is 0.0272, and at 272,001 it is 0.0544002; mutant M2 is killed. Every block has a contiguous comment that carries every §9.1 URL and 2026-10-10. OpenRouter has its comment-only citation. `PRICING_AS_OF` and the C010 lock moved together in one commit (`5d1ec7c4`). C009 shows only the baselined Mistral finding, and C010 is 0.
- **Placement is right for the records and the enum the spec named.** `SystemOneHost`, `OpenAIDecisionsWireQuestion` and `OpenAIDecisionsWireRequest` are frozen, slotted and validated in `vidbyte/lib/dataclasses/jev.py`, and listed in `__all__`. `DecisionAuthScheme` lives in `vidbyte/lib/enums/jev.py` and is exported. The seven wire constants live in `vidbyte/lib/constants/jev.py`. The `DecisionModelConfig.model: str | None` exception is documented in the docstring.
- **Each new adapter's `run_decision` keeps the single guarded, narrated `try` with the five typed branches.** Every `transport.request` passes `timeout_seconds`, headers stay out of `repr`, and cancellation propagates untouched (NFR-7).
